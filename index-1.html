About Us

Science-Driven Care, Human Touch

Founded in 2026, My Prime Diagnostic is Noida's newest and most technology-forward diagnostic laboratory. Our mission is simple: make accurate, affordable diagnostics accessible to every household.

  • Equipped with Siemens Healthineers, Beckman Coulter, and Abbott ARCHITECT analyzers — the same technology used in premium hospitals.
  • Our team of 40+ qualified pathologists, microbiologists, and lab technicians brings decades of combined expertise.
  • NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) certified with ISO 15189:2022 compliance.
  • Committed to sustainable healthcare — paperless reports, eco-friendly packaging, and solar-powered facility.
